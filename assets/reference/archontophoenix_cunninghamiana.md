# King palm, Archontophoenix cunninghamiana

Species key: `archontophoenix_cunninghamiana` (matches `palmcore.assets` species keys and `assets/library.yaml`). ASSET-001 reference sheet. All facts below cite a source in `## Sources`; `VERIFY:` marks anything not confirmed from a read page.

## Identification

Slender, single-stemmed feather palm with a prominent smooth green (or purple-green) crownshaft about 1.4 m long [WP-K, ANBG], a straight, uniform gray ringed trunk that swells slightly at the base [WP-K, GISD], and about 9-12 arching fronds [WP-K]. Fronds are dark glossy green above and lighter beneath, with a half-twist so the leaflet tips stand more vertical [WP-K, ANBG]. The inflorescence is lilac-pink and hangs below the crownshaft; fruit is bright red [WP-K, GISD]. Also called bangalow, piccabeen or Illawarra palm. 'King palm' is also the name of the related, larger `Archontophoenix alexandrae` [UF-21]; check that reference photos are `cunninghamiana` (leaflets more lax).

## Look-alikes

### King palm (`archontophoenix_cunninghamiana`) vs queen palm (`syagrus_romanzoffiana`)

Use the table in `assets/reference/syagrus_romanzoffiana.md`, section Look-alikes. In short: king has a smooth green crownshaft above a clean ringed gray trunk and sheds its own fronds. Queen has no crownshaft, a ragged crown base with hanging dead fronds, a looser, wider crown and a thicker, bulging, smooth gray trunk.

Also not to be confused with `Archontophoenix alexandrae` (Alexandra palm, also sold as 'king palm'): UF lists it larger (40 x 15 ft) with rigid leaflets held in one plane, versus this species at 30 x 12 ft with more lax leaflets [UF-21].

## Color notes

- Fronds at noon (sunlit): dark glossy green above, lighter beneath [ANBG]. Sampled hex (median of green-hue pixels, approximate): `#798543` from photo #4 (sunlit, includes yellow-green highlights). VERIFY: resample.
- Crownshaft: smooth, pale to mid green or purple-green [WP-K]. VERIFY: sample.
- Trunk: smooth, light gray with regular, slightly darker ring scars (photos #5, #6); paler than Canary or fan trunks.
- Fruit: bright red to orange-red [WP-K, GISD]; flowers lilac-pink.
