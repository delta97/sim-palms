# California fan palm, Washingtonia filifera

Species key: `washingtonia_filifera` (matches `palmcore.assets` species keys and `assets/library.yaml`). ASSET-001 reference sheet. All facts below cite a source in `## Sources`; `VERIFY:` marks anything not confirmed from a read page.

## Identification

Stout, columnar, single-stemmed desert palm with a rosette of large gray-green fan fronds. The trunk is brown when exposed but is usually hidden by a dense skirt of persistent dead fronds, which may reach the ground [WP-F, FEIS]. Long thread-like white fibres hang between the leaf segments (hence `filifera`) [WP-F, STAN]. The only palm native to California; planted in the southern part of the state, where it is rarer on streets than the Mexican fan palm and common at older sites (campuses, old estates, parks).

## Look-alikes

### California fan palm (`washingtonia_filifera`) vs Mexican fan palm (`washingtonia_robusta`)

| Test | filifera | robusta |
|---|---|---|
| Trunk | thick (0.3-0.6 m), parallel-sided [FEIS, STAN] | thin (0.2-0.3 m), flared base [UF-827, WP-R] |
| Skirt | heavy; down to the ground if untrimmed [FEIS] | bare on tall trees [UF-827] |
| Height | 9-15 m wild, to 18 m cultivated | 18-30 m |
| Crown | larger, open, gray-blue-green [WP-F, UF-826] | small, dense, bright glossy green [ASU] |
| Petioles | small blunt teeth, green bases [WP-F] | heavy spines, reddish bases [UF-827] |
| Fibres | conspicuous white threads [WP-F] | few |

Hybrids exist and have intermediate trunk thickness [ASU]. For modeling, a palm with a trunk visibly thicker than a lamp post and still wrapped in dead fronds is `washingtonia_filifera`. For the queen vs king pair see `assets/reference/syagrus_romanzoffiana.md` and `assets/reference/archontophoenix_cunninghamiana.md`.

## Color notes

- Fronds at noon (sunlit): dull gray-blue-green to gray-green, matte rather than glossy [WP-F]. Sampled hex (median of green-hue pixels; the filter drops the greyest pixels so the true average is greyer): `#505631` from the Balboa Park photo (#11 above). VERIFY: resample from a sunlit grove photo.
- Trunk: dark brown to gray, with a thick barklike rind [FEIS]; the bark close-up (#4) shows pale gray with deep vertical furrows.
- Skirt: straw to gray-brown, hanging vertically, darker when old. VERIFY: sample hex from photos #1, #2.
