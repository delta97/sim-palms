# Queen palm, Syagrus romanzoffiana

Species key: `syagrus_romanzoffiana` (matches `palmcore.assets` species keys and `assets/library.yaml`). ASSET-001 reference sheet. All facts below cite a source in `## Sources`; `VERIFY:` marks anything not confirmed from a read page.

## Identification

Feather palm with a slender smooth gray trunk and a crown of long, arching to drooping, plume-like fronds [UF-767; plan]. The trunk may have bulges [UF-767], and ring scars are visible in photos #10-11. No crownshaft: the leaf bases attach directly to the trunk top [UF-21; photo #12]. Orange fruit in large hanging clusters with heavy fruit drop [UF-767]. A very common SoCal residential and street palm. Former names `Cocos plumosa` / `Arecastrum romanzoffianum` [WP-Q], which is how old photos (e.g. #2, c. 1920) are labelled.

## Look-alikes

### Queen palm (`syagrus_romanzoffiana`) vs king palm (`archontophoenix_cunninghamiana`)

Both are slim-trunked feather palms; check from the top down.

| Test | queen | king |
|---|---|---|
| Crownshaft | none: leaf bases attach straight to the trunk top, dead fronds hang against the trunk [UF-21, UF-767] | prominent smooth green (or purple-green) crownshaft, about 1.4 m long [WP-K, ANBG] |
| Trunk | smooth gray, may bulge; 0.2-0.4 m [UF-767] | smooth gray with regular, close ring scars; slight swelling at the base; up to 0.3 m [WP-K, GISD] |
| Frond habit | long arching then drooping plumes; crown loose and broad (5-7.6 m) [UF-767] | arching, with a half-twist so distal leaflets stand nearly vertical [WP-K]; leaflets lax [UF-21] |
| Leaflets | 300-494 per frond, narrow [WP-Q]. VERIFY: whether leaflets lie in one plane | about 70-90 pairs, up to 1 m long [WP-K, GISD]; underside paler [ANBG] |
| Dead fronds | persist and hang until cut [UF-767] | fall off cleanly (crownshaft palms self-clean) [UF-21] |
| Crown width | 5-7.6 m | 3.7-8 m [UF-21, RHS] |

Modeler rule: look at the frond bases. A smooth green sleeve above a clean ringed trunk is king. Fronds springing from a ragged brown head with no sleeve, and dead leaves hanging below, is queen. 'King palm' is also the nursery name of `Archontophoenix alexandrae`, a similar and larger species; do not mix their photos or dimensions [UF-21].

## Color notes

- Fronds at noon (sunlit): mid green, lighter and feathery, often yellow-tinged in sun; leaflets droop so the underside shows. Sampled hex (median of green-hue pixels, approximate): `#57662E` from photo #6. VERIFY: resample.
- Trunk: smooth, light to mid gray [UF-767], paler than Canary or fan trunks.
- Fruit: bright orange [UF-767].
