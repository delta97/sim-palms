## Goal

`palmfx/offline/camera.py` turns SimConnect-style aircraft pose rows into camera poses and projects world points (lat/lon/height) to pixels and back. Projection masks, calibration and compositing all build on it.

## Context

- Math outline and conventions are in the plan_ref (A5). Signatures are in "Python interfaces".
- Sign conventions of the real SimVars are not known yet (FX-006 checks them). This module must take a `conventions` mapping (`pitch_sign`, `bank_sign`, `heading_sign`, each ±1, default 1) and work in canonical signs internally: pitch positive nose up, bank positive right wing down, heading true, clockwise from north.
- Heights are treated as MSL in both palms and poses (see Open questions in the plan about the datum).

## Steps

1. `LocalFrame(lat0, lon0, h0)`: ENU tangent frame. Use pyproj `Transformer.from_crs("EPSG:4979", "EPSG:4978", always_xy=True)` for geodetic to ECEF and its inverse; ECEF to ENU with the standard rotation matrix at (lat0, lon0). `to_enu` and `from_enu` are vectorised over N points.
2. `R_ned_from_body(heading_deg, pitch_deg, bank_deg)`: ZYX aerospace rotation `Rz(ψ)·Ry(θ)·Rx(φ)`.
3. Constants: `ENU_FROM_NED` and `CAM_FROM_BODY` (camera x = body right, y = body down, z = body forward).
4. `pose_from_simvars(row, frame, cam_offset_body_m, conventions, bias=None)`: apply signs, then bias (`d_heading_deg`, `d_pitch_deg`, `d_bank_deg`, `d_body_m`), compute `R_cam_from_enu` and camera position `c = p_aircraft + R_enu_from_body · (cam_offset_body_m + d_body_m)`. Optional row keys `cam_pitch_deg`, `cam_yaw_deg` (relative to body) are applied if present.
5. `Intrinsics` dataclass with `from_vfov(width, height, vfov_deg)`; `fx = fy = (H/2)/tan(vfov/2)`, `cx = W/2`, `cy = H/2`.
6. `project(points_enu, pose, K) -> (uv, z)`; `z ≤ 0` rows get `uv = nan`. `backproject(uv, z, pose, K) -> enu`.
7. Tests in `tests/unit/palmfx/test_camera.py`:
   - `to_enu(from_enu(x)) == x` within 1 mm over 5 km.
   - Level aircraft heading north, point 100 m north and 10 m below camera height: projects below the image centre on the vertical centre line, `z ≈ 100`.
   - Heading 90°: a point east of the camera projects to the centre.
   - Pitch +10° nose up moves a forward point down in the image; `pitch_sign=-1` with negated input gives the same result.
   - `backproject(project(p)) == p` within 1e-6 m for random points in front of the camera.
   - Camera offset: offset `[0, 0, -2]` (2 m up in body FRD) shifts projection as expected.
