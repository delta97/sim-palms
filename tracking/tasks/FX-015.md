## Goal

Three ways to turn per-frame inpainted fills into a sequence: `none`, `reuse` (warp the previous output) and `blend` (mix warped previous output with the new fill). These are the "simple fixes" the source plan's go/no-go allows.

## Context

- Flow method, fill, validity check and variant rules are in the plan_ref (A11).
- Flow inside the mask is replaced by flow interpolated from around it, because the blob moves differently from the background behind it.
- The flicker metric (FX-016) reuses `flow.py`; keep its functions pure and documented.

## Steps

1. `palmfx/offline/flow.py`: `to_gray(rgb)`; `dense_flow(prev_gray, cur_gray)` with `cv2.DISOpticalFlow_create(cv2.DISOPTICAL_FLOW_PRESET_MEDIUM)` giving current → previous flow; `fill_flow(flow, mask, dilate_px=7)` via `cv2.inpaint` per channel (flow scaled into a float-safe range, or use `scipy.interpolate.griddata` on the ring if `cv2.inpaint` needs 8-bit; document which); `warp(img, flow)` via `cv2.remap` (linear, border replicate); `fb_valid(f_cp, f_pc, tol_px=1.0, tol_rel=0.05)`.
2. `palmfx/offline/stabilise.py`: `stabilise(raw, fills, masks, variant, alpha=0.8, min_valid=0.8)` generator, recursive on its own previous output; outside the mask output = raw; refresh rule for `reuse`; records per-frame `valid_coverage` and `refreshed` in a side list.
3. `palmfx/offline/commands/stabilise.py`: `palmfx stabilise --session DIR --input <mask>-<backend> --variant none|reuse|blend [--alpha 0.8] [--min-valid 0.8]` writes `stab/<mask>-<backend>-<variant>[-a<alpha>]/NNNNNN.png` and `metrics/stab-<name>.json` (coverage, refreshes).
4. Tests: `test_flow.py`: pure horizontal shift of a textured image recovers flow within 0.3 px median; `fill_flow` inside a mask over a uniform-shift field gives that shift within 0.5 px; `fb_valid` marks an occluded region invalid. `test_stabilise.py`: on a static synthetic camera with noisy fills, `reuse` output inside the mask is constant after frame 0; `blend` variance decreases with alpha; outside-mask pixels equal raw; coverage below `min_valid` triggers a refresh.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Three variants available from the CLI; output dir names encode variant and alpha.
- [ ] Unit tests pass.
- [ ] Per-frame coverage and refresh flags written to metrics.
